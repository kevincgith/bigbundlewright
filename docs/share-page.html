Court bundles fit for a 大狀.

Drop in your documents and get back a hearing bundle that follows Practice Direction 5.6: indexed, paginated top right, hyperlinked and split into volumes. It all runs in your browser, so nothing is uploaded.

What it does

The tedious parts of a bundle, done the way the Practice Direction asks.

Index

Linked index and bookmarks

Every index row jumps to its page. Bookmarks nest under each section, with page numbers in the titles.

PD 5.6 para 4(3)

Numbers top right

Consecutive page numbers in the top right-hand corner, on a white box that shrinks the page rather than cover its text.

Volumes

Bundle A, B, C

Splits at 250 pages per volume without breaking a document across two files, plus a master index for the set.

PD 5.6 para 1

Late documents as 88-1

Once a bundle is issued its numbers are frozen. New documents slot in as 88-1, 88-2 and nothing else renumbers.

PD 5.6 paras 2 and 5

Core and transcript bundles

Prompts for a core bundle once documents pass 100 pages. Transcripts get their own bundle numbered T1, T2.

中英

Bilingual headings

Section titles and descriptions in English and Traditional Chinese, with Noto Serif TC built in so the index prints correctly.

PD 5.6 para 8

Lodging deadline

Counts back 72 hours from the hearing, skipping Saturdays, Sundays and Hong Kong general holidays.

Checks

Pages that need a look

Flags scans with no searchable text, encrypted files, landscape pages and anything sitting where the stamp goes.

Private by design

Your documents never leave your computer.

Bundles are full of privileged and personal material. Big Bundle Wright reads and writes PDFs inside the browser tab, makes no network calls while it works, and keeps no copy anywhere else.

  • No uploads, no trackingFonts and libraries ship with the app. Nothing loads from a CDN and nothing reports back.
  • A project file, not a copySaving keeps descriptions, dates and file fingerprints. Re-add the same PDFs and it checks they have not changed.
  • No hidden data in the bundleThe finished PDF carries no concealed metadata. Embedding the project for later editing is off unless you switch it on.
How it works

Three steps from a folder of PDFs to a bundle ready to lodge.

Add the documents

Drag in PDFs and photos. Dates and titles are read from file names, and images are fitted to A4.

Check the index

Sort by date, group into sections, mark each document's status and flag the core documents.

Build the bundle

Get every volume and the extras in one download, with a report of anything that needs attention.
